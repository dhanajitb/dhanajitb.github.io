Dhanajit Brahma

I am a postdoctoral associate at Duke University, where I work under the supervision of Prof. Ricardo Henao. I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Kanpur under the guidance of Prof. Piyush Rai. I have also worked as a research consultant at KAUST. My current research interests are developing frameworks for causal inference, test-time adaptation, agentic memory systems, and agentic AI.
